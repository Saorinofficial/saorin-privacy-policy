You may provide profile information when setting up your SAORIN account. This information is used to create and personalize your experience.

1.3 Onboarding and Personalization Information

During onboarding, SAORIN may collect information about your personal improvement preferences, including:

If you voluntarily provide additional information through features of SAORIN, we may process that information to provide the requested functionality.

We use the information we collect for purposes including:

  • Creating and maintaining your SAORIN account

  • Personalizing your SAORIN experience
