Account & Data Deletion

If you have a SAORIN account and want to delete your account and associated personal data, you can request account deletion without needing to have the SAORIN app installed.

How to request deletion

Send an account deletion request using the email address associated with your SAORIN account.

Request Account Deletion

What will be deleted

After your request is verified and processed, SAORIN will delete the account and personal data associated with your account, subject to any data that we are legally required or permitted to retain.

  • Your SAORIN account
  • Your profile information
  • Your username and associated profile data
  • Your onboarding and personalisation data
  • Other personal data associated with your SAORIN account

Verification

To protect your account and prevent unauthorised deletion, we may need to verify that the deletion request belongs to the account owner.

Processing

Once ownership is verified, the deletion request will be processed within the applicable timeframe described in the SAORIN Privacy Policy.

Need help?

If you have questions about account or data deletion, contact SAORIN using the same support email provided above.

For more information about how SAORIN handles personal data, please review our Privacy Policy .
